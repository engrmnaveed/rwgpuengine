    Controls

    Keys and taps: arrows or W A S D or the pad move, Enter or Space confirm, Escape cancel. A click or a tap is a tap; a drag or swipe of 12 frame pixels or more moves along the iso axes, or, in a game with no mover, pans: the ground follows the pointer.

    Camera: Q and E, or ⟲ and ⟳, turn a quarter turn; right-drag turns freely; middle-drag or Shift + right-drag pans; the wheel, a trackpad's pinch, - and = zoom; V or View switches between iso and perspective; Home goes back to the scene's camera. The camera's center stays over the ground.

    Touch: with two fingers the ground stays under them: move them together to pan, apart or together to zoom, twist them to turn, and the view settles on a quarter turn when they let go. A tap of two fingers switches the view; a tap of three fingers goes back to the scene's camera.

    Edit any project file and the player reloads it. URL: ?scene=…&input=…&frames=N runs N frames at once and pauses; the state hash matches rwgpu run. ?renderer=cpu draws with the CPU reference renderer.
