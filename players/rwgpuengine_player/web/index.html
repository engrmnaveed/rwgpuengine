    Controls

    Keys and taps: arrows or W A S D or the pad move, Enter or Space confirm, Escape cancel. A click or a tap is a tap; a drag or swipe of 12 frame pixels or more moves along the iso axes.

    Camera: Q and E turn a quarter turn; right-drag turns freely; middle-drag or Shift + right-drag pans; the wheel, a pinch (two fingers, or a trackpad), - and = zoom; V switches between iso and perspective; Home goes back to the scene's camera.

    Edit any project file and the player reloads it. URL: ?scene=…&input=…&frames=N runs N frames at once and pauses; the state hash matches rwgpu run. ?renderer=cpu draws with the CPU reference renderer.
