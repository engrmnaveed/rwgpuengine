# Open questions — decisions for the project owner

Things the code cannot decide. Each has a recommendation and what it blocks. When one is decided, record
the answer here (and an ADR if it is architectural), then remove it from the "open" list.

Built so far: step 1 (published, green on three systems) and step 2, the GPU-free 3D core ([ADR 0020](decisions/0020-render-list-camera-and-cpu-reference-renderer.md), 2026-11-26). | Q17 | **The reference renderer keeps ADR 0020's rules where two faces of one plane have different corners** (supervisor session, as a fix that makes the reference keep rules the owner approved in ADR 0020; reported to the owner on 2026-11-26). The GPU check found that a tile larger than one cell lost up to 76 % of its pixels to the ground, and that the part of a card below its anchor won or lost against its floor by chance. Now a level face takes its depth from its plane's equation, and a card's lower part shows over a floor in its plane. All ten playtests keep their image hashes. Notes with the measurements: [ADR 0020](decisions/0020-render-list-camera-and-cpu-reference-renderer.md#note-2026-11-26-faces-in-one-plane-and-cards-on-a-floor) and [ADR 0021](decisions/0021-wgpu-renderer-held-to-the-reference.md#what-the-check-found-in-the-reference-and-what-stays-undefined). **Still open inside it, for the owner:** doing the same for the sides of boxes changes one pixel of `tiny_town`'s `perspective.png`. | 2026-11-26 |
